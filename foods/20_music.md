# Music Capabilities

## Supported Operations
- **Play**: Play songs/videos from YouTube
- **Search**: Search for music on YouTube
- **Control**: Volume up/down, mute/unmute
- **Queue**: Add to playback queue (future)

## Music Platforms
- **YouTube**: Primary music source
- **Streaming**: Direct URL playback
- **Local**: Future support for local libraries

## Voice Commands
- "play believer" → Search and play on YouTube
- "play music" → Open music platform
- "volume up" → Increase system volume
- "mute" → Mute audio output

## Language Support
- English: "play believer", "search beatles"
- Hindi: "believer chalao", "beatles dhundo"
- Hinglish: "believer play karo", "beatles search karo"

## Future Enhancements
- Spotify integration
- Local library management
